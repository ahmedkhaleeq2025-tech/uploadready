How to convert JPG to PNG

Your image is converted in your browser and is not intentionally uploaded to an UploadReady server.

Why convert JPG to PNG?

PNG is useful when you want a lossless image format and can be useful for graphics, screenshots and images that need transparency. Converting a JPG to PNG does not restore image quality that was already lost in the original JPG.
