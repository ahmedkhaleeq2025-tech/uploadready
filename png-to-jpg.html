How to convert PNG to JPG

  1. Choose your PNG image.
  2. Click Convert to JPG.
  3. Preview the converted image.
  4. Download your JPG file.
Your image is converted in your browser and is not intentionally uploaded to an UploadReady server.

Why convert PNG to JPG?

JPG can produce smaller files for many photographic images. JPG does not support transparent backgrounds, so transparent PNG areas are rendered against a white background during this conversion.
