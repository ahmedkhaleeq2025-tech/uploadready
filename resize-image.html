How to resize an image

  1. Choose your JPG, PNG or WebP image.
  2. Enter the required width and height in pixels.
  3. Keep “Keep aspect ratio” enabled if you want the proportions preserved.
  4. Click Resize Image and download the result.
Your image is processed in your browser and is not intentionally uploaded to an UploadReady server.

Why resize an image?

Some websites, forms and applications require specific image dimensions. Resizing changes the pixel dimensions of an image without requiring you to upload it to a server.
